# Research template: proposal

Workspace-level Linear issue template « Research ». Applies the Type label
**Research**. Title prefix: none. Semantics and the test that decides
this type: [`../../references/ticket-types.md`](../../references/ticket-types.md).

Time-boxed; the deliverable is written, never shipped code.

```markdown
## In plain words, with an example

<What we do not know yet and why it matters. E.g. « We do not know which queue library handles our volumes. »>

## Question to settle

…

## Context

Why now.

## Leads to explore

* …

## Deliverable

A written decision, a proposal or a proof of concept, never code shipped to production. Time-box: X days.

## Where the answer will be written

Link to the lasting page or file.

## Decision roles

Who prepares, who decides, the review point, and the work actually blocked. If the answer is missing, keep the unknown and its next action; do not turn a proposal into a validated rule.
```
