# Blocker template: proposal

Workspace-level Linear issue template « Blocker ». Applies the Type label
**Blocker** and asks for an **Addressee** label. Title prefix: « Blocker: ». Semantics and the test that decides
this type: [`../../references/ticket-types.md`](../../references/ticket-types.md).

Makes the act asked for, and the date after which waiting costs, explicit.

```markdown
## In plain words, with an example

<What is stopped, for whom, and one concrete case. E.g. « Clients still see the old tracking screen: the new version cannot reach production until the infra team grants us access. »>

## What must be done

The act expected, in one sentence: an access, a deliverable, an intervention. The account or resource concerned.

## Who must act

Name, role or team (Addressee label). This ticket is created in the team that must act.

## Who is waiting

The waiting issues, linked as « blocked by » this ticket.

## When waiting starts to cost

DD/MM, and what waiting costs after that.

## Suggested way to do it

Your proposal, or « none ».

## Exchange so far

What was said, with the date and the people.
```
